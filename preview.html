How to read slopes: log-log slope ≈1 means linear scaling (BFS), ≈1.1 means n log n (mergesort), ≈0 means logarithmic/flat (binary search). Timings are relative on one machine — slopes are the signal, not absolute milliseconds.

Your 8-week plan (diagnose first)

Day 0
35 min ×3
Diagnose before studying. Three unseen problems, narrate aloud, record failure mode: recall / pattern / implementation / communication. Study only the largest gap.
Wks 1–2
fundamentals
arrays, strings, hash map, two pointers, binary search. Done = implement cold without lookup.
Wks 3–4
traversal + mocks
trees, graphs, BFS/DFS. Start mocks even if unready. Done = traverse anything + explain the choice.
Wks 5–6
advanced
heap, intervals, greedy, DP-start. Finish Blind75. Read DDIA 1–5. STAR bank 4→8 stories.
Wk 7
company styles
Target-company emphasis + 5–8 mocks total. Mistake-log re-solves from blank editor.
Wk 8
taper
No new material. Light re-solves, sleep 7–8h. Sharp beats exhausted.
Practice rule that actually works: 25–35 min cold → read approach only (never code first) → re-solve blank in 2 days. If you cannot, you recognized it — you did not learn it. 30 minutes of struggle before any AI help; AI critiques, never first-drafts.

FAQ — honest answers

How many problems? How long?
100–150 deeply (reproduce blank) beats 500 touched. 6–8 weeks @2h/day (Blind75) for most; 10–14 weeks (NeetCode150) for selective roles; ~80–100h for a 30-day sprint @2–3h weekday + 4–5h weekend. Timeline depends on diagnostic, not completion.
Is the original study plan enough alone?
No — it is the best free foundations map, not a full loop. Add timed unseen problems, company styles, communication reps, system design (mid+), SQL/frontend per role, and behavioral stories.
What about AI in interviews?
Attempt cold first. Use AI to critique, generate variations, quiz edges. In 2026 loops: more follow-ups, more “why”, more verification of numbers, more design judgment. Narrate everything.
What is NOT verified here?
No hiring-outcome RCT (we prove implementability/scaling/live-correctness, not offer rates). Single-machine slopes. Single-symbol anchor. Confirm per-company AI policy with your recruiter.
